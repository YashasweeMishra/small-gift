03 / FROM THE HEART

A FEW WORDS I OWE YOU

One thing I never really said...

FOR
SARTHU

A note from one hostel memory to another

I don't know what you think of me, or what place I hold in your life. Maybe I don't even know how you describe our friendship.

But one thing I know for sure is that you are one of the best people life has introduced me to.

We met at a hostel, without knowing that a random meeting would turn into something this meaningful.

I never expected that someone I barely knew would become someone whose presence, conversations, and existence would eventually mean so much to me.

We've had our own moments, our stupid conversations, endless laughter, random memories, and days that probably seemed ordinary at the time but now mean a lot.

I might not always express it, and I might not always find the right words, but I genuinely wish the best for you.

I hope life gives you everything you've ever dreamed of. I hope you find reasons to smile even on difficult days, strength when things get complicated, and people who appreciate you for exactly who you are.

I don't know where life will take us or how things will change with time.

But whenever we look back at these days, I hope we remember them with the biggest smiles.

Thank you for being a part of my story, Sarthuuu.

You aren't just someone I met at a hostel.

You're someone I'm genuinely glad I met.

And no matter how casually I say it, you'll always be one of my favourite people.

With lots of love,
Yours Lallaaaaa ♥
